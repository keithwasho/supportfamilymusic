Privacy Policy

SupportFamilyMusic.com is a simple site for the song "You're My Son." We keep the information we collect to a minimum.

What we collect

When you buy something, Stripe processes your payment and gives us your name, email address, and, for hats and tees, your shipping address and phone number. If you become a Bronze, Silver or Gold Supporter, the name you choose for the Patrons page is shown publicly there. We never see or store your card number.

How we use it

We use your details only to deliver your download, ship your order, and answer questions about it. Shipped items are printed and mailed by our fulfillment partner, which receives your shipping details for that purpose.

What we don't do

We do not sell or share your information with advertisers. The video on our home page is hosted by YouTube and only loads when you press play.

Your choices

To have your name removed from the Patrons page, or your information deleted, reply to your Stripe receipt email and we'll take care of it.
