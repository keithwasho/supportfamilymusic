Why This Song Exists

As a father, I wrote "My Son" to capture the deep love, pride, and responsibility parents feel toward their sons.

Every parent wants their child to grow up knowing they are loved, supported, and believed in.

This song was written for:

  • fathers and sons
  • mothers and sons
  • families everywhere

It is a reminder that our children are our greatest legacy.

Now we want to share this message with the world.

"You are my son; today I have begotten you."
PSALM 2:7
